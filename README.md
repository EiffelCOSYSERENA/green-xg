## What is Green-xG ?
Green xG is an open, federated testbed for energy-aware 5G/6G networks developed within the I-Site FUTURE (Université Gustave Eiffel) and the CHEDDAR Hub. It integrates and extends proven open-source components, including OAI (RAN/CN), Open5GS (CN), FlexRIC (near-RT RIC), Kepler (per-container power), Prometheus/Grafana (telemetry/visualization), to support reproducible, multi-site deployments (three sites today - Bordeaux, Paris, Lille - with straightforward extensibility). A carbon “oracle” (e.g., ENTSO-E) supplies real-time grid-intensity context, while the energy pipeline aligns measurements and KPIs for rigorous attribution. Together, these tools enable controlled A/B energy experiments, carbon-aware placement/scheduling (e.g., CU/UPF moves), per-pod power accounting, and end-to-end replay of experiments for verification and reuse.
